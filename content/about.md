# Professional Silicone Solutions Since 2000

**Xiamen Yidexin Silicone Rubber Industrial Co., Ltd** is a premier manufacturer specializing in OEM silicone products. With over 20 years of industry expertise, we have established ourselves as a reliable partner for global clients, providing high-quality silicone keypads, keyboards, and various rubber components.

### Our Strengths
- **20+ Years Experience**: Deep technical knowledge in silicone molding and design.
- **High Capacity**: Monthly production capacity of over 1,000,000 silicone keypads.
- **Quality Assurance**: Strict quality control systems and modern production facilities.
- **Global reach**: Formerly a 10-year Gold Supplier on Alibaba, serving customers worldwide.

Located in Xiamen, China, we operate from a state-of-the-art facility equipped to handle complex OEM/ODM projects from design to final production.
