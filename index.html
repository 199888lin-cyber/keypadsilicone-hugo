About Yidexin

20 Years of Precision Silicone Manufacturing

Founded in 2005, Xiamen Yidexin Silicone Rubber Co., Ltd. has grown into a leading professional manufacturer of silicone rubber products in China. Our facility covers over 5,000 square meters and is equipped with advanced compression molding, liquid injection molding (LIM), and CNC tooling machines.

We specialize in custom silicone keypads, medical-grade components, and high-performance rubber seals. With our in-house mold design and manufacturing team, we provide rapid prototyping and cost-effective solutions for clients across medical, automotive, industrial, and consumer electronics sectors.
